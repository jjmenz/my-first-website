Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your dog's stay.

When can I drop off and collect my pets?

You can drop off and collect anytime between 8am and 5pm. If you collect prior to midday, that day is free.

What should we bring?

Bring along your pet's vaccination certificate and any prescription diets or medications. Feel free to bring along your own bedding and coats, however we supply everything your little ones need on their stay.

What if my pet requires medications?

If your pet requires medication while they're staying with us, please bring along the medication and prescriptions. There is no additional charge for administering.

Do you supply flea and tick treatment?

No, flea and tick treatment is to be put on 2 days prior to arrival.

Can I visit my pets while they are staying at the resort?

Of course, you can! We can also provide information on dog friendly areas around Noosa. Our regular drop-off and pickup times apply.

Does the Pet Taxi service my area?

Our Pet Taxi services all the Noosa and Sunshine Coast regions. We also provide transport to and from our sister boarding facility, Gympie Pet Motel.

Do you take bookings over Christmas?

We take bookings for Christmas all year. Spaces do fill quickly; feel free to call for more information on 07 5447 1516.

Are discounts available?

Yes! We offer pension discounts, longer stay discounts, and discounts when there's a family of boarders.
